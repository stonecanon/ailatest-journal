Refund Policy

Annual plans have a cooling-off window; monthly plans can be cancelled anytime. Email us for order issues.

Last updated: 2026-07-16

1. 年付订阅

  • 14 天无理由退款：首次购买年付 Pro / Max 后 14 天内，可申请全额退款。
  • 超过 14 天，年付当期一般不退，但可随时取消自动续费，权益用至当期结束。
  • 若因忘记取消导致年付续费，可在续费后 14 天内联系我们；若新周期几乎未使用付费权益，我们可全额退还该笔续费。

2. 月付订阅

  • 可随时取消，当期结束后不再扣款；已开始的月份一般不退。
  • 若因我方故障导致服务在当月实质不可用，可申请退还或抵扣该月费用。

3. 试用

限时试用通常无需预留支付方式，试用结束自动回到 Free，不产生扣款，故无「试用退款」。

4. 重复扣款与差错

重复扣款或与展示价格不符的账单，核实后全额退回。

5. 如何申请

  1. 发邮件至 contact@ailatest.org，主题写「退款申请」。
  2. 写明注册邮箱与订单号（支付方收据上）。
  3. 我们通常 2 个工作日内确认；批准后退回原支付方式，到账时间取决于银行（常见 5–10 个工作日）。

6. 法定权利

本政策不限制你所在地消费者保护法赋予的不可放弃权利。方案说明见 订阅页。

1. Annual subscriptions

  • 14-day money-back: first annual Pro / Max purchase may be fully refunded within 14 days.
  • After 14 days, the current annual period is generally non-refundable; you can cancel renewal and keep access until period end.
  • If an annual renewal charged because you forgot to cancel, contact us within 14 days of renewal—if paid features saw no material use, we refund that renewal in full.

2. Monthly subscriptions

  • Cancel anytime; access continues to month end. Started months are generally not refunded.
  • If a fault on our side made the Service substantially unusable that month, we refund or credit it.

3. Trials

Trials typically require no payment method and drop to Free without a charge—nothing to refund.

4. Billing errors

Duplicate charges or amounts that don’t match the listed price are refunded in full after verification.

5. How to request

  1. Email contact@ailatest.org with subject “Refund request”.
  2. Include account email and order number from the receipt.
  3. We confirm within ~2 business days; approved refunds return to the original method (banks often take 5–10 business days).

6. Statutory rights

Nothing here limits non-waivable consumer rights. Plans: Pricing.
