Guide

怎么选？

Which plan?

按使用场景快速判断。网站查刊三档均可；徽章显示「试用」是限时体验，不等于付费 Pro。

Pick by workflow. Website search is on every plan. A “Trial” badge is a limited-time perk — not paid Pro.

Free只在网页查刊

看 IF、分区、收录与详情即可，无需插件深度徽章或云收藏。

Pro日常刷刊 + 插件

Scholar / PubMed 需要中科院徽章，并希望有云收藏与 AI 荐刊额度。

Max风险信号 + 导出

需要预警 / 撤稿信号、导出到文献库，以及更高 AI 与设备额度。

FreeWeb-only lookup

Check IF, quartiles, indexes and journal pages without extension depth or cloud lists.

ProDaily browsing + extension

Need CAS badges on Scholar / PubMed, plus cloud favorites and AI pick quota.

MaxRisk signals + export

Want warning / retraction signals, export to reference managers, and higher AI / device limits.

Notes
教育价与支付
· 机构邮箱（.edu / .edu.cn / .ac.* 等）登录后可解锁教育价；普通个人邮箱无法支付教育价。
· 已用机构邮箱登录时，标准价会锁定，请点「教育价」按钮，避免按全价付款。
· 年付通常比月付更划算。支付由安全收银台完成；退款见 退款政策。
· Skill / API 与 MCP 目前为公测；可匿名调用，登录后可在账户中创建 API Key，接口和额度可能调整。
Education pricing & checkout
· Institutional emails (.edu / .edu.cn / .ac.*, etc.) unlock education pricing after sign-in.
· When signed in with an institutional email, standard-price buttons are locked—use the edu CTA.
· Yearly is usually better value. Checkout is secure; see the refund policy.
· Skill / API and MCP are public beta. Anonymous calls are allowed; signed-in users can create an API Key. Limits and fields may change.
中国区一次性产品与支付
· Pro / Max 为一次性 365 天访问权，不自动续费；到期后可重新购买。
· 填写邮箱、姓名和账单信息后继续，Creem 收银台会按产品、地区、设备和账单信息显示可用方式；符合条件时可选择支付宝，也可能显示 Apple Pay / Google Pay，具体以 Creem 收银台为准。
· 功能、额度、退款与服务中止安排以条款及退款政策为准。
China one-time products & checkout
· Pro / Max are one-time 365-day passes with no auto-renewal; users may buy again after expiry.
· After entering your email, name and billing details, continue to Creem; the checkout shows available methods based on the product, location, device and billing details. Alipay may appear, and Apple Pay / Google Pay may also be offered; the checkout is authoritative.
· Features, quotas, refunds and service changes follow the Terms and Refund policy.
