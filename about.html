平台能力

期刊检索与比较

按刊名、ISSN、学科、索引和分区筛选期刊，集中比较投稿前最常用的信息。

指标与风险信号

展示 JCR IF 与 Quartile、中科院分区、索引收录、OA / APC、审稿周期、预警与撤稿提示。

智能荐刊

输入论文题目、摘要或关键词，结合研究主题与期刊画像生成候选期刊清单。

插件与小程序

浏览器插件在 Scholar、PubMed、知网等页面展示期刊徽章；微信小程序提供移动端查询与收藏入口。

What the platform provides

Search and compare journals

Filter by title, ISSN, subject, index and ranking to compare the information most useful before submission.

Metrics and risk signals

View JCR IF and quartiles, CAS zones, indexing, OA / APC, review cycles, warnings and retraction cues.

AI journal matching

Enter a paper title, abstract or keywords to generate candidate venues from research topics and journal profiles.

Extension and mini program

The browser extension shows journal badges on Scholar, PubMed and CNKI; the WeChat mini program supports mobile lookup and saves.

关于智能荐刊

用户可以输入论文题目、摘要或关键词，平台结合研究主题、学科分类、期刊收录情况、分区指标和投稿条件，生成候选期刊清单，并按照优先推荐、备选期刊等层次展示。

智能荐刊用于缩小检索范围和辅助比较，不构成录用、投稿成功或学术评价承诺。正式投稿前，请核对期刊官网的收稿范围、APC、投稿政策和风险信息。

AI journal matching

Users can enter a paper title, abstract or keywords. The platform combines research topics, subject areas, indexing, ranking signals and submission conditions to produce candidate venues, shown as priority picks and alternatives.

AI matching narrows the search and supports comparison; it is not a promise of acceptance, successful submission or academic evaluation. Verify the journal’s scope, APC, policies and risk information before submitting.

关于纠错与合作

如果期刊编辑部、出版社或研究人员发现期刊元数据、收录状态或评价信息存在错误，可以通过联系页面提交公开来源和更正依据。平台将根据可核验的官方资料进行复核和更新。

Corrections and cooperation

Editors, publishers and researchers can report journal metadata, indexing or rating errors through the contact page with a public source and supporting evidence. We review and update information when it can be verified against an official source.

数据说明

AILatest Journal 聚合 Web of Science、JCR、中科院分区、EI、Scopus、DOAJ、MEDLINE、ESI、OpenAlex、CCF、ABDC、ABS、科协目录及国内核心期刊目录等公开数据和元数据。不同数据源的发布时间和更新周期并不相同，平台采用版本化、批量同步的方式更新，并在页面标注数据来源、版本和截止时间。

下表列出主要数据类型、来源机构与本站当前采用版本 / 截止日。著作权归原发布机构；本站仅做聚合检索与展示。「截止时间」指上游公开版本或本站最近一次纳入该源的日期，不是实时爬虫时刻。

Data sources & cutoffs

AILatest Journal aggregates public data and metadata from Web of Science, JCR, CAS zones, EI, Scopus, DOAJ, MEDLINE, ESI, OpenAlex, CCF, ABDC, ABS, CAST lists and domestic core-journal directories. Sources have different release schedules, so we use versioned batch updates and show the source, edition and cutoff where available.

Copyright stays with the original publishers. We only aggregate for search and display. “Cutoff” means the upstream public edition or the date we last ingested that source—not a live crawl.
