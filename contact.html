What to write about

Product & extension

Feature ideas, bugs, badge issues. Include browser / extension version and a page URL when possible.

Data corrections

Subject “Data correction” with journal title / ISSN, wrong field + correct value, and an official source link.

Account & billing

Sign-in, trial, Pro / Max, edu pricing, missing entitlements. See Pricing and Refunds.

Business

Partnerships, API, content—use subject “Business”.
